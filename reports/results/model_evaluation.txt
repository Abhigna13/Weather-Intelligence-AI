Interpretation
--------------

MAE:
Measures the average absolute difference between the actual
and predicted temperature values.

MSE:
Measures the average squared prediction error and gives more
weight to larger errors.

RMSE:
Represents the typical prediction error in the same unit as
the target temperature (°C).

R2 Score:
Measures how well the model explains the variation in the
target temperature.
